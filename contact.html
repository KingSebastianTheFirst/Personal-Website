Get in touch

I welcome contact from people working on or curious about sustainable finance and related questions.

This includes researchers and academics interested in collaboration, students exploring thesis or dissertation ideas, journalists looking for research context or commentary, practitioners and policymakers working on sustainable finance, ESG, or climate risk, and anyone with a genuine question about the field.

The best way to reach me is by email at s.rink@fs.de. I try to respond to all substantive enquiries, though response times may vary during busy periods.

Talking to journalists

I am generally happy to speak with journalists writing about sustainable finance, climate risk, ESG, or related policy topics. Please briefly describe your piece and deadline when reaching out.

Speaking and events

I occasionally speak at conferences, workshops, and events on sustainable finance topics. For speaking enquiries, please include the event details and topic.
