Digital Garden 🌿

An evolving collection of ideas, concepts, and connections from sustainable finance research.

What is a digital garden?

A digital garden is a set of living notes — somewhere between a research wiki and a personal notebook. Unlike a finished article or a blog post, garden notes are not complete or polished. They grow over time as ideas develop. Some are early sketches; others have been refined through papers, teaching, and conversations with colleagues.

The nodes below represent concepts central to my research. They are connected to papers, related themes, and each other. You are welcome to explore in any order. The status system shows how mature each note is:

🌱 Seedling — early sketch or short note 🌿 Growing — developed with links 🌳 Evergreen — mature and stable
