Teaching

Courses, seminars, and executive education in sustainable finance — at Frankfurt School and beyond.

I have been teaching sustainable finance since 2019 across a range of formats: graduate-level university courses, executive MBA programmes, professional certifications, and custom practitioner trainings. Teaching connects directly to my research — I aim to bring current findings from the field into the classroom and to learn from the practical experience students and participants bring with them.

University Courses

Financing Sustainability and Transformation

Since 2024 · M.Sc. Finance · Frankfurt School of Finance & Management · Rated excellent (2024 & 2025)

A graduate-level course examining how financial systems can support the sustainability transition. Topics include climate-related financial risk, sustainable lending, ESG data, green bond markets, transition finance, and the role of regulation. The course bridges academic research and practitioner perspectives, drawing on empirical findings alongside industry case studies.

Sustainable Finance

Since 2020 · EMBA · Università Bocconi (Finance Week at Frankfurt School of Finance & Management)

A module within the Executive MBA Finance Week, designed for senior professionals with backgrounds across business and finance. The course provides an intensive introduction to sustainable finance — covering ESG integration, climate risk, green investment products, and the evolving regulatory landscape in Europe.

Executive Education

I contribute to a range of professional development programmes at Frankfurt School, covering sustainable finance from introductory to specialist level. These programmes serve practitioners from banking, asset management, insurance, and the public sector.

Other Teaching

Lecturer, VÖB-Akademie

Since 2022 · In-person and online

Lectures on sustainable finance topics for banking professionals through the VÖB-Akademie (Bundesverband Öffentlicher Banken Deutschlands). Covers regulatory developments, climate risk integration, and sustainable product design for practitioners in German public banking.
